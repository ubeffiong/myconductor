§ 4 · Coverage

Locus callability across the cohort

Coverage is the load-bearing precondition for every call. A drug whose defining loci are not callable cannot be reported as susceptible — this is the structural guarantee that absence of evidence is never rendered as susceptibility.

Variants prioritised for laboratory validation

Myconductor does not predict resistance for these variants. It ranks them by the evidence that would resolve them — structural context, conservation, coverage, and catalogue proximity. The output is a research queue, not a clinical call.

Efflux, regulatory, and non-catalogue mechanisms
